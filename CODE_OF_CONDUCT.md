# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

ProjectBase contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to make participation respectful and harassment-free. Be constructive, assume good intent while addressing impact, accept feedback, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, publishing another person's private information, sustained disruption, and retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

These expectations apply in repository discussions, reviews, issues, community spaces, and public interactions where someone represents ProjectBase.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile: <https://github.com/AndrianBalanescu>. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details. If the repository owner is involved in the concern, use GitHub's platform reporting tools.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, commits, issues, and other content that violates this policy. Depending on severity and context, they may issue a private warning, impose a temporary restriction, or permanently exclude a participant. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible, but absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

Enforcement decisions should be made in good faith, consistently, and with regard for the safety of the community. Retaliation against reporters or participants in an investigation is prohibited.

## Attribution

This is a project-specific conduct policy informed by common open-source community standards. It is not presented as an unmodified copy of another code of conduct.
