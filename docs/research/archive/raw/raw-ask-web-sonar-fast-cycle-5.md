## Practical hardening notes for a “ProjectBase”‑style deployment

These points are generic but aligned with what Plane and other modern trackers do:

* Design around a lean, well‑tuned database and cache (e.g., Postgres + Redis), minimizing expensive joins and ensuring good indexing on issue, cycle, and assignment fields.  
* Keep your real‑time layer (WebSockets/long polling) lightweight and avoid blocking calls on user interactions to approach Linear‑like UI responsiveness.  
* Aim for predictable resource usage similar to Plane’s reported idle footprint and scale horizontally rather than relying on single large instances.  
* Treat keyboard‑driven workflows and latency budgets as first‑class features: optimize common operations (create issue, change status, assign, move across boards) until they feel instantaneous on typical hardware.
