## Notes that matter for purchasing

* Linear’s free plan is generous on members but hard-capped at 2 teams and 250 issues, which is the main practical limit for small teams [Source](https://linear.app/pricing) [Source](https://checkthat.ai/brands/linear/pricing).
* Plane’s Community Edition is the strongest self-hosted option in this set for feature breadth plus AGPL availability, but some advanced capabilities may require paid commercial licensing even on your own infrastructure [Source](https://www.sollmannkann.com/project-management-and-notes/best-plane-review/) [Source](https://freealternatives.to/plane/free-plan) [Source](https://plane.so/blog/plane-vs-clickup-which-should-you-choose-in-2026) [Source](https://plane.so/open-source).
* Taiga and Focalboard remain the most clearly self-hostable/open-source-friendly options in spirit, but the gathered results did not provide enough current pricing and limit data to state exact 2025–2026 seat costs confidently.
